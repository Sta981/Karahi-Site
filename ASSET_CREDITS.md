# Asset acknowledgments

## Visual design reference

The restaurant storefront layout and palette were adapted using the [Karahi House menu on Hey Eats](https://app.heyeats.shop/store/karahi-house/shop) as a visual reference.

## Images

The restaurant logo, banner and a selection of dish photographs were sourced from that public store page. Additional food photographs came from the sources recorded in [the image manifest](app/static/img/food/sources.json), including Junction London Indian Street Food, Food Faith Fitness, The Royal Peacock Indian Restaurant, Royal Rasoi and Wikimedia Commons.

The manifest records each image's original URL, source page, original label and local filename. Some related-dish photographs are used as representative images for the sample menu.
